🥫 Food Drive

Supporting the Lynnwood Food Bank

What to Bring

We're partnering with the Lynnwood Food Bank this year to collect non-perishable food and essential household items. Bring donations to the event and drop them in our collection boxes. Every item makes a difference for local families in need.

Canned Goods

  • Vegetables
  • Fruit
  • Soups
  • Beans
  • Tomato sauce
  • Baby food

Dry Goods

  • Pasta
  • Dried beans & lentils
  • Oats
  • Flour
  • Stuffing mix
  • Spices & seasonings
  • Cereal

Proteins & Staples

  • Canned tuna or chicken
  • Peanut butter
  • Ramen (12-pack)
  • Cooking oil
  • Gluten-free pasta
  • Cultural cooking ingredients

High-Priority Items

  • Rice
  • Feminine products
  • Toilet paper (6+ rolls)
  • Laundry detergent
  • Baby wipes (multi-pack)

Critical Need

  • Diapers (20+ count)
  • Infant formula
  • Pet food
