About the Event

Skate For a Cause 2026 is back — and this year we're introducing a Wish Tree for Seattle Children's Hospital. Come skate with us and help make a difference for children and families in need this holiday season.

How the Wish Tree Works

1

At the event, you'll find a Christmas tree decorated with gift cards. Each card features a specific item that Seattle Children's Hospital is in need of, along with an image, description, and price tag.

2

Pick a card from the tree that speaks to you and bring it to one of our volunteers.

3

Purchase the tag at the listed price. We'll take care of buying the item and delivering it directly to Seattle Children's Hospital on your behalf.

Who We're Supporting

🏥 Seattle Children's Hospital

Seattle Children's Hospital is one of the top children's hospitals in the country, providing care to children across the Pacific Northwest and beyond. Through our Wish Tree, every item purchased goes directly to the hospital to support patients and their families during the holiday season.
