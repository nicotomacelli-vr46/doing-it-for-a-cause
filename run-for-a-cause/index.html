About the Event

Skate For a Cause 2026 is back — and this year we're donating to Seattle Children's Hospital and the Lynnwood Food Bank. Come skate with us and help make a difference for children and families in need this holiday season.

Who We're Supporting

🏥 Seattle Children's Hospital

Seattle Children's Hospital is one of the top children's hospitals in the country, providing care to children across the Pacific Northwest and beyond. Through our Wish Tree, every item purchased goes directly to the hospital to support patients and their families during the holiday season.

Learn More →

🥫 Lynnwood Food Bank

The Lynnwood Food Bank serves thousands of families across Snohomish County. This year we're collecting non-perishable food and household essentials at the event to help stock their shelves and support local families in need.
