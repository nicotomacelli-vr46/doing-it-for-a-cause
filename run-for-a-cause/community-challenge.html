🏆 Community Challenge

Compete for a cause — teams & clubs welcome

How It Works

Sports teams, clubs, and community organizations — this one's for you. Compete to collect the most points by bringing donated items to the event. The team with the highest score wins the grand prize!

1

Collect items before or at the event. Use the point system below — higher-need items are worth more points.

2

Send your team reps to Skate For a Cause on December 12, 2026 at Lynnwood Ice Center with your donations.

3

The organization with the most points wins! Points are tallied on-site at the event.

Point System

2 pts
Canned vegetables, canned fruit, canned soups, canned beans, tomato sauce, baby food, stuffing mix
3 pts
Pasta, dried beans, lentils, canned tuna or chicken, oats, flour, spices & seasonings
5 pts
Ramen (12-pack), peanut butter, cooking oil, cereal, gluten-free pasta, cultural cooking ingredients
10 pts
Rice, feminine products, toilet paper (6+ rolls), laundry detergent, baby wipes (multi-pack)
20 pts
Diapers (20+ count), infant formula, pet food

🏅 Grand Prize

The winning organization takes home a $50 pizza party for your team — or, if you prefer, a $50 donation made directly to your organization. Your choice!
